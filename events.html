The Spaces

Where Your Event Lives

The entire property is available for your exclusive use. Here are the primary gathering spaces.

Outdoor Grounds

The main gathering space — open to the Hill Country sky, set across the meadow with panoramic bluff and lake views. Ideal for ceremonies, dinners under the stars, or all-day celebrations.

  • Up to 200 guests seated
  • Flexible layout (ceremony, banquet, cocktail)
  • String lighting throughout
  • Natural shade structures
  • Bluff overlook access

Covered Reception & Bar

A covered event structure with built-in bar, flexible seating, and direct flow from the outdoor grounds. Designed for receptions, cocktail hours, presentations, and keynote dinners.

  • Covered structure with open sides
  • Built-in bar area
  • AV hookup available
  • Dance floor area
  • Climate-appropriate for all seasons
