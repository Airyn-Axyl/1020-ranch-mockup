Driving Directions

1020 Ranch is located in the Texas Hill Country, just outside of Burnet. The drive is scenic and easy from all major Texas cities.

From Austin

~60 minutes northwest via TX-29 W through Liberty Hill and Burnet. A beautiful drive through rolling Hill Country.

From San Antonio

~90 minutes north via US-281 N through Johnson City and Marble Falls. Stop in Marble Falls for lunch on the way up.

From Dallas / Fort Worth

~3 hours south via I-35 S to TX-29 W. Worth the drive.

From Houston

~3.5 hours northwest via US-290 W through Austin. Consider staying overnight at the ranch.
