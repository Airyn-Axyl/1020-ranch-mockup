A Weekend Together

Wedding Weekend Timeline

Here's how a typical weekend flows when your party stays on-site.

Friday

Arrive & Settle In

Guests check into tiny homes starting at 2 PM. Welcome drinks on the lawn. Rehearsal dinner on-site or in Burnet. S'mores by the fire pit under the stars.

Saturday

The Big Day

Morning coffee by the lake. Bridal party gets ready on-property. Ceremony on the bluff or meadow. Cocktail hour flows into reception. Dance under the string lights. Late-night campfire wind-down.

Sunday

Farewell Brunch

Slow morning. Brunch for the whole crew on the covered patio. Photos at golden hour. Guests check out by noon. No rush - just one last morning together.
