How the Weekend Flows

Our Weekend Wedding Retreat is designed so the celebration never feels rushed. Here's how most couples structure their three days at the ranch.

Day 1
Friday — Arrival
Day 2
Saturday — The Wedding
Day 3
Sunday — Farewell
Time Friday Saturday Sunday
Morning — Bridal party gets ready in on-site suites. Vendors arrive and begin setup. Slow morning. Guests enjoy the property, lake, and bluff views at leisure.
Afternoon Guests check in to tiny homes (2pm). Explore the property and settle in at your pace. Ceremony begins. Family portraits on the bluff. Cocktail hour in the meadow as sun sets. Farewell brunch (optional). Final goodbyes, memories made. Check-out by noon.

Frequently Asked

1020 Ranch accommodates up to 200 guests for ceremony and reception. For overnight stays, the six on-site tiny homes sleep between 2–4 guests each (up to 20+ guests on-property). Additional nearby lodging options in Burnet are available for larger wedding parties.
We operate an open vendor policy — you are free to bring any licensed and insured caterer, florist, photographer, DJ, or planner. We do maintain a list of vendors who have worked on-property and know the layout well, which we're happy to share as a starting point.
Yes. Licensed bartenders and catering companies may serve alcohol on-property. BYOB arrangements may be available through specific packages — please ask about your options during your tour.
All six tiny homes include full bedding, linens, private bathrooms, kitchenette, and outdoor seating areas. They are designed for comfort and quiet — a true retreat for your closest guests. Tiny home access is included in the Weekend Wedding Retreat package and available as an add-on for other packages.
Yes, the property includes ample parking for all guests. For larger weddings, we recommend coordinating with your planner on any shuttle logistics from lodging in town, though the majority of couples find on-site guest parking is sufficient.
The ceremony space includes a partial shade structure. For inclement weather, we work with couples in advance to identify covered contingency configurations on-property. A formal weather contingency plan is part of every wedding contract.
Peak season dates (spring and fall) typically book 12–18 months in advance. Summer and winter dates tend to have more availability. We recommend scheduling a tour as soon as your date is in mind — there are no commitment requirements for touring.
